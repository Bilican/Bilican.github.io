About Deformable Convolution with Motion Estimation

This visualization demonstrates the concept of deformable convolution in deep learning, where convolution operations adapt their sampling grid based on the input. Here, we use motion vectors between two frames to determine how the convolution kernel should deform.

The process follows these steps:

  1. Source & Target Frames: We start with two distinct frames with known differences.
  2. Motion Estimation: We calculate motion vectors between the frames (predetermined for visual appeal).
  3. Deformable Convolution: The standard convolution grid is offset by the motion vectors, allowing the operation to adapt to the movement.
  4. Result: The convolution follows the motion flow, effectively transforming the source toward the target.

This technique is valuable in video processing, object detection with deformation, and other computer vision tasks where standard convolutions struggle with geometric transformations.
